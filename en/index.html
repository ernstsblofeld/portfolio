The studio

Mediamax takes a film from the shoot to the final image. Three crafts under one roof:

  • Compositing and visual effects

    3D integration in Blender, tracking, clean-up and element replacement in Fusion.

  • Colour grading and restoration

    Looks, shot matching and restoring older footage, like Silence, restored for its 20th anniversary.

  • Aerial cinematography

    DJI drones, from flight planning to the graded image.

Cameras: RED, Blackmagic 6K and URSA. Editing, grading and finishing: DaVinci Resolve. Visual effects: Fusion and Blender.

Olivier G. Burri

Telling stories with technique, not for its own sake.

I have been making films for more than twenty years, from Silence (2005, restored in 2025) to Hybris and Fichtl's Lied. With Mediamax, I bring visual effects and colour grading to my own shoots. I shoot on RED and Blackmagic, with Tilta rigs and DJI drones, then handle editing, grading and finishing in DaVinci Resolve. And I share what I learn along the way.
